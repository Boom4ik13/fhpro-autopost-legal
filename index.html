FHPRO Autopost

An internal publishing tool of FLY HIGH PRODUCTION LLC

FHPRO Autopost is an internal tool used by the video production studio FLY HIGH PRODUCTION LLC (FHPRO) to publish videos and Shorts produced by the studio to YouTube channels owned by the studio or its clients, and to track how these videos perform.

What the application does

  • Uploads videos and Shorts to a YouTube channel that the channel owner or manager has explicitly connected via Google sign-in.
  • Sets the title, description, tags, thumbnail, captions and scheduled publication time of these videos.
  • Reads statistics of the videos it published (views, likes, comments, and YouTube Analytics reports) to build performance reports for the channel owner.

Who uses it

The application is not offered to the public. It is operated only by FHPRO staff on behalf of channels that have granted access. There is no public sign-up.

Use of YouTube API Services

FHPRO Autopost uses YouTube API Services. By connecting a channel, users agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy, our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
